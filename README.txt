Milk Diary - simple mobile-friendly HTML app

1. Download and extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome.
3. Select today or any earlier date.
4. Choose 0, 1 or 2 packets, then tap Save entry.
5. To change a saved day, select a different quantity and tap Update entry.
6. The app automatically calculates the month at Rs.33 per packet.
7. Monthly payment confirmation is disabled until the last day of that month. It becomes available on that day and remains available when viewing that completed month later.

Future dates are disabled. You can browse previous months. English only.
Data is stored locally in the current browser/device; it does not sync between devices.
